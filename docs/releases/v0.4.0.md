# edownloader 0.4.0 — Pinterest, X, Instagram, TikTok and Bilibili, and the AppImage is back

**Paste a Pinterest board, get the whole board; paste a photo post, get its photos.** Bilibili videos start again. And the Linux AppImage, withdrawn from 0.3.0, starts on current distributions.

## New

- 📌 **Pinterest pins, boards and board sections.** Paste a pin, a board, a section of a board or a `pin.it` share link, from pinterest.com or any country site. Pictures and GIFs come at the highest resolution Pinterest offers, videos as MP4, every page of a story pin and every picture of a carousel. A board lands in a folder named after it, and the row counts its files as they arrive. Until now, only Pinterest videos downloaded, through yt-dlp; picture pins and boards failed.
- 🔁 **Run a board again for what is new.** Every file name ends with a short `[key]` from Pinterest's own file name, so edownloader skips what is already in the folder. If a few files fail, **Retry** fetches only those.
- 🔒 **Your secret boards too.** Import cookies from a browser signed in to Pinterest to download the boards your account can open. Public pins and boards need no account.
- 🖼️ **Photos from X and Instagram.** A post with photos now downloads them at the highest resolution the site offers, along with the post's videos when it mixes both; several files land in a folder named after the post, numbered in order. Until now these posts failed with "No video could be found". Posts with videos only still go through yt-dlp, with your format preset.
- 🎞️ **TikTok photo posts.** A slideshow downloads every picture, in order, and its sound, the MP3 TikTok serves, as is (only the sound with the **audio** preset). Short share links (`vm.tiktok.com`, `vt.tiktok.com`) work too. TikTok videos are unchanged.
- 📺 **Bilibili videos that would not start.** Bilibili turned yt-dlp away with an HTTP 412 error; edownloader now reads Bilibili itself, with your preset: the height cap, H.264 in MP4 mode when the video offers it, or the audio alone. `?p=` picks one part of a multi-part video; with **playlist** on, every part lands in a folder. Without signing in, Bilibili generally limits quality to 480p; with cookies from a browser signed in to Bilibili, edownloader asks for the higher qualities your account has access to. `b23.tv` links work too.

## Fixed

- 🐧 **The AppImage starts again on current distributions.** It carried graphics libraries from the build system (Ubuntu 22.04), older than the ones your graphics drivers expect, so the drivers did not load and no GPU was found. It now uses your system's own Vulkan, Wayland and keyboard libraries, like the `.deb` and `.rpm` do, and the release workflow refuses an AppImage that bundles them.

## Install

**Windows:** already on 0.3.0? edownloader updates itself. New here? Download **`edownloader_0.4.0_x64-setup.exe`** below and run it.

**Linux:** pick the package for your system below. If you got the 0.3.0 AppImage before it was withdrawn, download the 0.4.0 one by hand: an AppImage that does not start cannot update itself. AppImageUpdate, Gear Lever and AppImageLauncher can still update it.

| System | x86_64 | arm64 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint… | `edownloader_0.4.0_amd64.deb` | `edownloader_0.4.0_arm64.deb` |
| Fedora, openSUSE… | `edownloader-0.4.0-1.x86_64.rpm` | `edownloader-0.4.0-1.aarch64.rpm` |
| Anything else | `edownloader_0.4.0_x86_64.AppImage` | `edownloader_0.4.0_aarch64.AppImage` |

`SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the checksum of every package. The `.AppImage.zsync` files are for AppImage update tools. `latest.json` is what the updater reads.
